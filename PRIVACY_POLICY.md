# Privacy Policy — A Habit Challenge

*Last updated: October 1, 2026*

A Habit Challenge (package name `com.chyavanphadke.ahabittracker`, published by Chyavan Phadke) is built to be fully private and offline.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app:

- does **not** connect to the internet (it does not request the `INTERNET` permission),
- does **not** collect, transmit, or sell any personal data,
- contains **no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs**.

## Where your data lives

All app content — habits, contracts, completions, settings — is stored **only on your device** in a local database (Room/SQLite). Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

## Permissions we request and why

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Reminders you configure, so you don't miss your habits |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Re-schedule reminders after a device restart |

The app's home-screen widgets read only your locally stored data to display progress on your launcher.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
